How we work

From idea to finished furniture

A transparent process at every stage — you always know where your order stands.

01

Consultation & measurement

I visit your space, we discuss your needs, and I take precise measurements — free of charge.

02

Design & 3D visualization

I put together a layout and 3D render, and we choose materials, colour and hardware together.

03

Approval & quote

We confirm the final design and exact price — no hidden extras later on.

04

Production

Carcasses and fronts are made in-house, with quality control at every step.

05

Delivery & installation

Careful delivery and on-site assembly, with every joint checked before we leave.

06

Warranty & support

5-year warranty and after-installation support — from door adjustments to hardware replacement.
